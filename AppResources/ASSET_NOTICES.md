# MP4Flow asset notices

## Typeface

The bundled `NotoSansSC-Variable.ttf` identifies itself as Noto Sans SC and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The complete licence text is included as `Fonts/OFL.txt` inside the application bundle.

## Interface and installer artwork

The interface marks for film, hard drive, plus, lightning, and status are geometric SwiftUI drawings in `Sources/FastConvert/DesignSystem.swift`. The DMG background is generated by `Scripts/create-dmg-background.swift`. Neither asset uses a third-party icon library or stock video footage.

`AppIcon.icns` is distributed as an MP4Flow project asset. Before release, the maintainer must retain records that establish the right to distribute it and must not replace it with a third-party brand asset without adding the required notice and licence.

## README demonstration

`docs/assets/mp4flow-demo.gif` is programmatically drawn by `Scripts/create-readme-demo.swift`. It contains a product illustration rather than footage from a third-party video.
